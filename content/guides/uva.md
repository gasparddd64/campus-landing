# UVA International Student Guide

A practical guide for international students arriving at the University of Virginia in Charlottesville.

## Banking

Opening a US bank account is one of the first things you should do after arriving. You'll need it to receive your stipend, pay rent, and build US credit history.

**Best options for international students:**
- **Chase Bank** — Offers a student checking account with no monthly fee. Branch at 100 E Main St, Charlottesville. Bring your passport, visa, I-20, and university acceptance letter.
- **Bank of America** — Campus Banking Center on University Ave. Similar requirements. Good for ATM access nationwide.
- **Wells Fargo** — Also accepts ITIN instead of SSN for account opening.

**Tips:**
- You can open an account without a SSN if you bring your passport, I-20, and proof of address (a letter from the housing office works).
- Consider **Wise** or **Revolut** for international transfers while you wait for your US account to be active.
- Apply for a **Deserve EDU Mastercard** — a credit card designed for international students with no SSN required.

## SSN (Social Security Number)

You need a SSN to work legally in the US, file taxes, and open certain financial accounts.

**Who qualifies:**
- Students with a work authorization (on-campus jobs, CPT, OPT, etc.)
- Students with a fellowship or stipend

**Process:**
1. Wait at least 10 days after arriving in the US before applying (SSA needs to verify your entry).
2. Get a letter from the International Studies Office (ISO) confirming your work authorization.
3. Go to the Social Security Administration office at **905 E Market St, Charlottesville** (Mon–Fri, 9am–4pm).
4. Bring: passport, visa, I-94 (print from cbp.gov/i94), I-20, SSO letter, and proof of address.
5. Receive your SSN card by mail in 2–4 weeks.

**Without a SSN:** You can use an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) for banking and tax purposes. Request it via Form W-7 with the IRS.

## Phone Plan

Getting a US number is essential — many services and apps require one for verification.

**Best options:**
- **Mint Mobile** — $15/mo for 5GB data, unlimited talk & text. Works on T-Mobile network. Buy a SIM online, no SSN required.
- **T-Mobile International SIM** — Good if you want a temp plan right away before deciding.
- **Google Fi** — Great for travel; supports international use.
- **Cricket Wireless** — Affordable prepaid, no credit check.

**Tip:** Bring an unlocked phone from home or buy a used one at UVA's student marketplace.

## Transport

Charlottesville is a small city. Here's how to get around:

**On Foot / Bike:**
- The UVA Grounds are very walkable.
- Download the **Zagster** or **Lime** app for bike share.

**Local Bus (CAT — Charlottesville Area Transit):**
- Free for UVA students with your UVA ID on most routes.
- Route 7 connects the Rotunda area to downtown.

**UTS (University Transit Service):**
- Free shuttles around Grounds, including to hospital, law school, business school.
- Download the **DoubleMap** app to track buses in real time.

**Rideshare:**
- Uber and Lyft are available, but less frequent late at night.

**Car:**
- Not needed unless you live far off-Grounds. Parking on Grounds is expensive.
- **Zipcar** is available on campus if you need a car occasionally.

**To/from DC, Richmond, etc.:**
- **Amtrak** station is 0.5 miles from Grounds (walk or quick Uber). Trains to DC (~2.5h, ~$20–40).
- **FlixBus** and **Greyhound** depart from downtown.

## Housing

Charlottesville housing is competitive, especially close to Grounds.

**On-Grounds (recommended for your first year):**
- Apply through UVA Housing as early as possible. Graduate students can apply for on-Grounds apartments.
- Contact: housing@virginia.edu

**Off-Grounds popular areas:**
- **Wertland / Rugby** — Walking distance from Grounds, very popular with students. ~$800–1,200/mo for a room in a shared house.
- **Barracks Road / Ivy** — Quieter, need a car or bus.
- **Downtown Mall** — Lively area, slightly further.
- **Belmont** — Up-and-coming, affordable, 10-min bike ride.

**Finding housing:**
- **UVA Off-Grounds Housing** portal: housing.virginia.edu
- **Facebook Groups:** "UVA Off-Grounds Housing" and "UVA International Students"
- **Campus Landing listings** — your cohort's own housing board
- **Craigslist Charlottesville** — use with caution, always visit in person

**⚠️ Scam warning:**
Never wire money or pay a deposit before seeing the place in person (or having someone you trust visit). Legitimate landlords will never ask you to pay via gift card, Zelle, Venmo before you've signed a lease and toured.

**Lease tips:**
- Standard leases run Aug 1 – Jul 31.
- Utilities often not included — budget $80–150/mo extra.
- Renters insurance (~$15/mo) is often required and always recommended.

## Common Scams

Be aware of these common scams targeting international students:

**Housing scams:**
- Listings on Craigslist/Facebook with below-market prices that require a deposit before viewing.
- Landlord claims to be out of the country and can't show the property.
- Requests for payment via wire transfer, gift card, crypto, or Zelle to a stranger.

**IRS/SSA phone scams:**
- Someone calls claiming to be IRS or SSA and says you owe money or your visa will be canceled.
- **The IRS never calls you without first mailing a notice. Hang up.**

**Job scams:**
- Too-good-to-be-true remote jobs that pay in advance and ask you to send money back.
- "Secret shopper" roles that ask you to wire money.

**Visa/immigration scams:**
- "Immigration consultants" (not licensed attorneys) offering to fix your visa.
- Always use UVA's International Studies Office (iso.virginia.edu) for visa questions.

**Tech support scams:**
- Pop-ups claiming your computer is infected. They will charge hundreds. Close the tab.

**If you are a victim or witness a scam:**
- Report to UVA Police: (434) 924-7166
- Report to the FTC: reportfraud.ftc.gov
- Talk to the ISO office for immigration-related incidents.
